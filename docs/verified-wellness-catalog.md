Names, addresses, course names and facility types are factual references. Descriptions are original concise summaries. No invented reviews, ratings, prices, opening hours, attendance or health outcomes. Forest therapy bases may encompass multiple routes: their published municipality is retained and explicitly distinguished from entrances and meeting points. Missing coordinates remain null; no map pin or GeoCoordinates is emitted. Operator map centers are labelled representative.

Sources: Forest Adventure operator park directory, Gokurakuyu operator directory (closed shops excluded), Ryusenji operator directory (eight stone-bath facilities), and Forest Therapy Society individual certification pages. Five bath facilities with incomplete address extraction were excluded. BIOTOPIA and Akasawa were deduplicated against the original catalog. The import never overwrites existing records or reviews and runs transactionally/idempotently.

Frontend: source filter and address search, category/area counts from the database, local browser shortlist (50 IDs maximum), shareable shortlist URLs, session-scoped comparison across pages (three IDs maximum). Saved lists are browser-local, not account synchronized. Filter and shortlist pages are noindex; detail pages and category pages retain crawlable content and sitemap coverage.
